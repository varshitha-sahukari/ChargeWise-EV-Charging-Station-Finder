## 2. Critical Adjustments & Enhancements

### A. Contact Info Parsing
*   **The Problem:** The original resume had hyperlinks bound to anchor words like "LinkedIn" and "GitHub". Many ATS parsers strip hyperlinks, leaving behind only the word "LinkedIn", which means the recruiter never gets your profile link.
*   **The Fix:** Spelled out the clean URLs in text (e.g., `linkedin.com/in/sahukari-varshitha`, `github.com/svarshitha49`). ATS systems will now parse the text itself and associate these platforms with your profile.

### B. Summary & Title Integration
*   **The Problem:** The original summary was a block of text that lacked structural keywords.
*   **The Fix:** Optimized the summary to include high-demand role titles: **"Software Engineer | Full-Stack Developer"** and specialized domain terms like **"Microservices Architecture"**, **"Database Design"**, and **"Distributed Systems"**.

### C. Projects: The Action-Scale-Outcome Formula
To make your resume competitive, every project description was rewritten to demonstrate technical impact, scale, and results:

1.  **ChargeWise (EV Charging Station Finder)**
    *   *Added Metrics:* "implemented 15+ RESTful API endpoints" (Scale), "spatial indexing to achieve sub-100ms response times" (Performance/Optimization), "real-time visualization of 1,000+ EV charging stations" (Scale).
2.  **E-Commerce Microservices Platform**
    *   *Added Metrics:* "partitioning the application into 3 independently deployable services (Catalog, Orders, User Auth)", "reducing configuration/environment errors by 40% using Docker & Docker Compose" (Efficiency/Quality).
3.  **Consistent Hashing Visualizer**
    *   *Added Metrics:* "minimizing data redistribution by 85% during node scaling" (Mathematical/System Optimization impact).

### D. Leadership & Responsibility Quantification
Even non-technical sections need numbers to prove impact:
*   **Roobaroo Dance Society:** Added scale metrics: **5,000+ attendees**, **15+ team members**, and **budget/schedule management**.
*   **National Cadet Corps (NCC):** Quantified leadership span: **commanded and trained a platoon of 30+ cadets**.

### E. Keyword Vocabulary Additions

*   `Vite` (modern React building tool).

*   `Spatial Indexing` & `Geospatial data` (key backend concepts).
